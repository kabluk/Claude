Our Story

Born in Armenia.
Now in Glendale.

Since 2017, Dragon Garden has been bringing the rich, authentic flavors of China and Japan to three locations across Armenia — known for introducing the traditional hot pot experience to the country.

The Glendale location at 216 N Orange Street is our first step onto American soil. We brought everything — the 200+ dish menu, the sushi bar, the hot pot tradition — and added an exclusive lounge with premium hookah and live DJ nights.

One restaurant. Every night, a different experience.
