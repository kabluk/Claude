Hero Scene Concepts (3 options)
A — Dragon's Breath Hotpot
Extreme close-up of a cast-iron hotpot. Dark kitchen, single overhead light. Steam rises slowly, broth bubbling. Chopsticks lower a Wagyu slice in. Camera drifts forward. Deep reds and golds.
B — Midnight Sushi Ritual
A sushi chef's hands press a roll on black stone. Dramatic side-lighting. Gold sauce drizzles in slow motion. Camera pulls back slowly to reveal the full counter. Black + gold + white marble.
C — Dragon Lounge at Night
Aerial tilt-down into a dark lounge table. Hookah smoke curls. A cocktail glass with ice catches neon red light from above. Camera slowly circles. Cinematic nightlife atmosphere.
