First call

Three rules to tell them on the first call

They will be frightened too. Keep the call short, stay calm, and tell them these three things — they matter most in the first hours.

  1. 1

    Do not sign anything.

    Tell them not to sign any document they are handed until a lawyer they trust has read it — even if they are told it will help them or get them out faster. Signing can give up important rights.

    Tell them: “Do not sign. Wait for the lawyer.”
  2. 2

    Say little on the phone.

    The call may be recorded. Tell them not to talk about their case, how they entered, or the details of their situation on the phone. A calm “I’m okay, we are working on it” is enough for now.

    Keep it to: “I’m okay. We are working on it.”
  3. 3

    Give them one phone number to memorize.

    They may lose their phone and all their contacts. Give them one number — yours, or a lawyer’s — and have them repeat it out loud until they know it by heart.

    Have them repeat the number until they know it.

Please read this

This keeps them safe — it is not legal advice

These are general safety points for the first hours, not legal advice about your specific case, and they do not replace an attorney. Once you have a lawyer you trust, follow their guidance instead.
