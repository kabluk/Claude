    First call & safety

    The three rules to tell them, and how to protect your money.

    Three rules for the first call

    Keep the call short and calm. Tell them these three things.

    1 · Do not sign anything until a lawyer you trust has read it.
    2 · Say little on the phone — the call may be recorded.
    3 · Give them one phone number to memorize.

    Check an attorney before you pay

    Ask directly whether they are a licensed attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative — then confirm it yourself on the official free list.

    Warning signs

    Treat any of these as a reason to stop: a guaranteed result or release, a promised “same-day release”, prepaid document packages sold before anyone reviews the case, or a “notario” who is not a licensed attorney.

    Detention Navigator is an information and organization tool. It is not a law firm, does not give legal advice, and does not promise any outcome. For legal decisions, consult a licensed attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative.
