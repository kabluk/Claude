How to protect your money

How to spot a scam

Some people take money from families at the worst moment. These are the warning signs — and how to check someone before you pay.

  • ⚑

    Anyone who guarantees a result or a release. No one can promise that.

  • ⚑

    A promised “same-day release” or a specific date they will be out.

  • ⚑

    Prepaid document packages sold before anyone has looked at the case.

  • ⚑

    A “notario” or consultant who is not a licensed attorney or an accredited representative.

How to check someone before you pay

Ask directly: “Are you a licensed attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative?” Then confirm it yourself on the official U.S. Department of Justice list — it is free and public.
